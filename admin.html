How to get Ranked

The Jordanian Minecraft Tierlist ranking is determined by active testers using standardized test matches. To request a test:

  1. Join our official Discord server.
  2. Go to the #request-test channel.
  3. Select the gamemode you'd like to test in and ping an available Tester.
  4. Complete a Best-of-5 (BO5) or Best-of-9 (BO9) duel. The tester will decide your tier based on your mechanical skill, positioning, and overall performance.

How Ranking Points are Calculated

Tiers are organized from Tier 1 (highest) to Tier 5 (lowest). Each tier awards points toward the Overall Leaderboard:

Tier 1: Peak mechanics, national-level competitors.

HT2 (30 pts)MT2 (25 pts)LT2 (20 pts)

Tier 2: Extremely advanced fighters, tournament regulars.

HT3 (10 pts)MT3 (8 pts)LT3 (6 pts)

Tier 3: Solid mechanics, reliable execution.

HT4 (4 pts)MT4 (3 pts)LT4 (2 pts)

Tier 4: Intermediate capability, active player base.

HT5 (2 pts)MT5 (1.5 pts)LT5 (1 pt)

Tier 5: Entry-level competitive PvP.
